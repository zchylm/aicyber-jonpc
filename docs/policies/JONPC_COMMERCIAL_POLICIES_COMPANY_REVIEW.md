## 1. Terms of sale

1. All prices are in Australian dollars and include GST unless clearly stated otherwise.
2. An order is accepted only when JON.PC confirms cleared payment. An automated order acknowledgement does not by itself mean that JON.PC has accepted an order.
3. Product images may be illustrative. The final supplied system must match the confirmed specification or an expressly accepted equivalent. JON.PC must obtain the customer's approval before making a material substitution.
4. JON.PC may cancel and refund an order if there is a genuine pricing error, stock becomes unavailable, the order cannot lawfully be fulfilled, or fraud is reasonably suspected. The customer will be notified promptly.
5. Nothing in these policies excludes, restricts or modifies rights and remedies that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law (ACL).

## 2. Payment

### Stripe

- Stripe card payments are confirmed by the payment provider. JON.PC does not store full card numbers.
- An order and any eligible cashback place are locked only after Stripe reports a successful payment.
- A pending, failed, abandoned or cancelled payment does not secure a cashback place.

### Bank transfer

- The customer must use the unique payment reference shown at checkout.
- A bank-transfer order is reserved for the period displayed at checkout. Customer notification that a transfer was made is not proof of payment.
- JON.PC accepts the order and locks any eligible cashback place only after an administrator verifies cleared funds in the company bank account.
- The qualifying time is the bank's recorded receipt time entered by the administrator, not the time the customer clicks “I have made the transfer”.

### Refund destination

Refunds are returned to the original payment method where reasonably possible. A bank-transfer refund may require verified account details. JON.PC will not request online-banking passwords, card PINs or one-time security codes.

## 3. Shipping, dispatch and pickup

1. **Melbourne metro delivery:** A$25 including GST for an eligible complete-system order to a postcode in the published JON.PC Melbourne metro list. The list is based on Australia Post's Melbourne metropolitan service area and is checked at checkout.
2. **West Melbourne pickup:** Free collection from 205 Kensington Road, West Melbourne VIC 3003 by appointment after JON.PC sends a ready-for-collection notice. Identification and the order reference may be required.
3. **Outside the flat-rate area:** JON.PC will provide a delivery quote before taking payment. The A$25 rate must not be shown or charged for an ineligible postcode.
4. **Dispatch commitment:** JON.PC will build, test and dispatch the system within 7–14 business days after cleared payment, unless the product page or accepted quote states a different timeframe.
5. Carrier transit time begins after dispatch and is not part of the 7–14 business-day dispatch commitment. Tracking details will be provided when available.
6. If JON.PC becomes aware of a material delay, it will contact the customer with an updated estimate and available options. The customer's ACL rights remain unaffected.
7. Risk passes on delivery to the nominated address or collection by the customer or their authorised representative. Title passes after full cleared payment.

### Melbourne metro flat-rate postcodes

The production implementation uses the Australia Post Melbourne metro postcode schedule. Operations must review that source periodically. The website should provide a postcode checker rather than printing the full list on the main page.

## 4. Delivery confirmation and problems

1. JON.PC records delivery using the carrier's delivery event or an administrator-confirmed pickup.
2. The customer can choose **Confirm received** or **Report a problem** from the order page.
3. If no problem is reported, the order is operationally marked received 48 hours after recorded delivery. This starts the cashback payout workflow where applicable.
4. The 48-hour process is not a limitation period and does not remove any warranty, repair, replacement, refund or other ACL right.
5. Customers should report visible transit damage or a missing parcel as soon as practicable so JON.PC can preserve carrier evidence and assist quickly.

## 5. Returns, cancellations and refunds

### Faults and consumer guarantees

- Customers are entitled to remedies under the ACL when goods fail to meet a consumer guarantee.
- For a major failure, the customer may be entitled to choose a refund or replacement. For a non-major failure, JON.PC may first choose to repair the product within a reasonable time.
- JON.PC will reimburse reasonable return costs when it confirms that the product has a covered fault or otherwise where required by law.

### Change of mind and custom systems

- **Company decision required:** approve the exact change-of-mind period, condition requirements and any exclusions before publishing.
- Recommended position: an unbuilt order may be cancelled for a full refund. After parts have been specially procured or assembly has started, a custom-configured system is not eligible for a change-of-mind cancellation unless JON.PC agrees. This does not affect ACL rights.
- Cancellation requests must be made through support and are effective only when acknowledged by JON.PC.

### Refund timing

Approved refunds will be initiated promptly. Bank and card processing times are controlled by the relevant financial institution. JON.PC will provide confirmation when the refund has been initiated.

## 6. Three-year parts / two-year return-to-base warranty and repairs

1. Complete JON.PC systems include a three-year parts warranty and a two-year return-to-base warranty from the recorded delivery date, in addition to the customer's ACL rights. ACL rights may continue beyond those periods depending on the product, price and circumstances.
2. The warranty covers defects in materials, supplied components and workmanship under normal intended use.
3. It does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, misuse, unauthorised modification, malware, data loss, cosmetic wear, or damage caused by external power/network equipment, except where the law requires otherwise.
4. Installing ordinary user-upgradeable components does not automatically void the whole warranty. JON.PC may decline coverage only where the modification or component caused or contributed to the problem.
5. Before returning a data-bearing device, the customer should back up and remove sensitive data where possible. Repair may require data access, reset or replacement. JON.PC will handle personal information in accordance with its Privacy Policy.
6. JON.PC will provide an assessment and available remedy. No-fee repair/replacement and reasonable freight support apply where required by the ACL or this warranty.

## 7. Custom PC build terms

1. Online selections and budget ranges are a request for review, not a final price or offer.
2. JON.PC checks compatibility, availability and current supplier pricing before issuing a final written quote.
3. No payment can be taken for a custom build until the customer accepts the current final quote.
4. The accepted quote must identify the final specification, GST-inclusive total, delivery or pickup charge, and dispatch commitment.
5. **Company decision required:** choose the quote-validity period. Recommended default: 48 hours because component pricing and availability can change quickly.
6. A component may be substituted only with the customer's express approval if the change is material. Any price change requires a revised quote and acceptance.

## 8. Founder Cashback terms

### Offer

1. Fifty eligible order places are available in each calendar month.
2. Each month runs from 12:00 am on the first day to 11:59:59 pm on the last day in the **Australia/Melbourne** time zone. Unused places do not roll over.
3. Each eligible paid order uses one place. A customer may receive cashback on more than one eligible order; every order consumes a separate monthly place.
4. Cashback is **5% of the eligible product subtotal actually paid**. Delivery charges, discounts and refunded product amounts are excluded. There is no advertised dollar cap.
5. The remaining monthly places and the 5% calculation must be shown before payment. Availability shown before payment is indicative until payment is successful or cleared funds are verified.

### When a place is secured

- Stripe: the payment-success timestamp reported by Stripe.
- Bank transfer: the bank receipt timestamp verified and recorded by an administrator.
- A customer click, checkout creation, transfer report, card authorisation attempt, failed payment or pending payment does not secure a place.
- If all 50 places for that Melbourne month have been secured, the order is not eligible and cannot be added retrospectively if a place later becomes available.

### Refunds and released places

- A full refund voids the cashback. If the refund occurs in the same Melbourne calendar month in which the place was secured, the place is returned to that month's availability.
- A partial refund recalculates cashback as 5% of the remaining eligible product subtotal.
- If cashback has already been paid, the handling of a later refund must be disclosed in the refund confirmation and applied lawfully. **Finance/legal decision required:** approve whether any overpaid cashback is deducted from the refund or separately recovered.

### Payout

- Eligible cashback becomes payable after delivery is confirmed (by the customer or automatically after 48 hours) and no cancellation, refund, chargeback or reported delivery/product issue remains open.
- JON.PC initiates the payout within one business day after it becomes payable. Financial institution processing time is additional.
- Stripe orders are refunded to the original payment method where supported. Bank-transfer orders require verified payout details.
- Cashback is a separate promotional payment and does not reduce the GST invoice amount unless accounting advice requires a different treatment.
